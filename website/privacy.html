Privacy

Pebbles is made by HellenicDev. This page says what we keep, in plain language. Last updated 14 November 2026.

Waitlist

If you join the waitlist, we send your email address to MailerLite and store it there, so we can write when there is news. We don’t ask for your name. We don’t sell the list. Each of those emails has MailerLite’s unsubscribe link. You can also ask us to remove you by opening an issue on GitHub.

The waitlist form talks to our endpoint at api.hellenicdev.eu, which passes the address on to MailerLite. That endpoint may count requests from your network address for about a minute, in memory, to slow down abuse. We don’t write that address down.

Touches

A touch — “thinking of you”, a hug, or “got it” — is end-to-end encrypted between Pebbles. The server does not keep a history of touches, and we can’t read them.

Crash reports

The app may offer anonymous crash reports. They stay off unless you opt in. They are for fixing faults, not for advertising, and they are not tied to your waitlist email.

This website

The site doesn’t use tracking cookies or analytics. Your theme choice, light or dark, stays in local storage on your device. Nothing else from a visit is stored by us.

Contact

Questions and requests go through GitHub: hellenicdev/pebbles.

Pebbles is open source. Firmware, app, and server.
