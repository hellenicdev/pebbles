How it works

No threads. No history. A touch, and the person you mean feels it.

  1. Keep a small group

    Pair up to five Pebbles. The people you actually want to reach.

  2. Tap, or hold

    A tap means “thinking of you”. A long hold is a hug.

  3. They feel it

    Theirs glows in your colour, warms gently, and chimes. A touch back means “got it”.

What a Pebble does

A quiet object. Warmth, light, and a buzz — and nothing stored afterwards.

  • Your colour

    Their Pebble glows in the sender’s colour, so a touch has a face.

  • Gentle warmth

    A small heater, capped around 42°C. Warm in the hand, not hot.

  • Buzz and chime

    A haptic buzz and a soft chime. Easy to notice, easy to live with.

  • Quiet hours

    Choose when a Pebble stays still.

  • Charging dock

    Wireless charging, with an LED ring on the dock.

  • Private by design

    Touches are end-to-end encrypted. There is no message history. Firmware, app, and server are open source.

FAQ

What is a Pebble?

A small device you keep nearby. Touch yours, and someone else’s lights up and warms. It is not a phone, and it does not carry a conversation.

How many people can I reach?

A group holds up to five Pebbles.

What does a touch mean?

A tap is “thinking of you”. A long hold is a hug. Touching back sends “got it”. That is the whole language.

How warm does it get?

The heater is capped around 42°C. It is meant to feel like a warm hand, and then stop.

Can anyone read a touch?

No. Touches are end-to-end encrypted, and the server does not keep them. There is no history to browse later.
